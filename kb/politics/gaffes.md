# The rapid-response archive

Maintained by the office. He calls it the archive of successes, reads it as
evidence of momentum, and has asked twice for it to be printed and bound. It has
not been printed.

## 14 March

Opened the new grain silo by describing it as "the freshest building I have ever
smelled". The silo is empty. A reporter asked whether that was a comment on
agricultural policy. He said "exactly" and left.

## 22 March

Attended the flood defence opening. The flood defences were not yet installed.
Spoke for six minutes about "water, and how it finds a way" while standing in the
dry trench. The photograph is now on a mug.

## 3 April

Announced a minister. No ministry exists for the portfolio. The minister, who was
present, accepted the post and has since been performing duties nobody can
identify. He says she is doing very well.

## 19 April

Asked to name the third bridge. Named two bridges and a bakery. Later clarified
that the bakery "is a bridge in the economic sense".

## 2 May

Held a silence for a minute. Held it for four. Staff describe the additional three
minutes as "the strongest stretch of the campaign so far".

## 11 May

At a ribbon-cutting, cut the ribbon and then, on being told it was a reopening,
cut it again, noting that "the second cut is the one that counts".

## 27 May

Was asked for a number. Provided a number. The number was later described by the
office as "a position, not a statistic".
