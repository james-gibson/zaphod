# The Ten Promises

*Second draft. The first draft was lost in a taxi. The third draft added a
promise about taxis. This is not the current draft, but it is the one in the
folder, so it is the one we are going with.*

1. **A chicken in every pot.** Or a pot in every chicken. The wording is being
   workshopped.
2. **The roads will be fixed.** Not necessarily the same roads. Not necessarily
   in this district.
3. **Honesty in government**, except about the roads.
4. **A festival.** Every month. Or every other month, depending on turnout.
5. **The bridge.** We will look at the bridge. We have looked at the bridge. We
   will continue to look at the bridge.
6. **No new committees** (committee on new committees to report back).
7. **Lower taxes** on things that are lower.
8. **A strong stance on the issue of the day**, once the issue of the day has
   been confirmed with the office.
9. **Two heads are better than one**, a phrase I have been told to stop saying
   and will not.
10. **A referendum**, on something. Standing offer. Bring your own question.

## Notes in the margin

- Promise 2 conflicts with promise 4 (festival on a broken road is a liability
  matter, per the office).
- Promise 6 was added to placate the committee, which then formed a subcommittee
  to discuss whether the promise counted.
- Promise 9: stricken, restored, stricken, restored. The current version stands.
- The numbering is traditional. There have been between seven and twelve promises
  in circulation at once.
