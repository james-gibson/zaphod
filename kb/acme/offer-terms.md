# Project BEETLE — indicative terms

*Strictly private and confidential. Not an offer capable of acceptance. Not, in
fact, capable of being understood by all recipients.*

| Term | Indicative position |
| --- | --- |
| Acquirer | ACME Corporation (Mergers and Very Acquisitions) |
| Target | the Beeblebrox Group |
| Consideration | 1,200,000,000 credits |
| Form of consideration | 40% scrip, 40% deferred, 20% promises |
| Promises | non-transferable, one (1) per shareholder |
| Completion | Tuesday, subject to the car park |
| Exclusivity | 30 days, or until somebody mentions a caravan |
| Break fee | the sandwiches |
| Governing law | the local paper |

## Conditions precedent

1. Due diligence, limited to the building and not the people in it.
2. Confirmation that the Group owns the Group.
3. The Chairman's approval of the car park, in writing, in the minute.
4. No further letters consisting solely of a photograph and the word "no",
   unless the photograph is a good one.

## Advisors

- **ACME:** a team of eleven, one of whom has read the file.
- **Target:** none retained. The Chairman has retained a caravan.

*This is fiction. No transaction is proposed, and no figure above corresponds to
anything.*
