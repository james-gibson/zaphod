# Beeblebrox Group — board minutes (extract)

**Present:** the Chair, two directors, the Company Secretary, and the Chairman
(hereafter "the Chairman"; the Chair has asked for the distinction to be noted).
**Apologies:** the third director, who has not been told there is a board.

---

**1. The acquisition.**

The Chair explained, for the fourth time, that ACME proposes to buy the Group.
The Chairman asked whether this would affect the car park. The Chair said that
the car park is, in a sense, the reason for the meeting. The Chairman said "good
car park, that" and asked for the minute to record his approval of the car park.

**2. Structure of the transaction.**

ACME's advisors presented a diagram. The Chairman described the diagram as "a
lovely bit of work" and asked who had drawn it. The Company Secretary recorded
that the diagram had three arrows, one of which pointed inward, and that this was
considered significant.

**3. Consideration.**

The Chairman asked whether "consideration" meant the sandwiches. It was explained
that consideration means the price. The Chairman said the sandwiches were the
best part of the morning and asked for that to be minuted as well. It was.

**4. The caravan.**

Noted: the caravan is not an asset of the Group and is not in scope. The Chairman
objected that the caravan is the most valuable thing he owns, which the Chair
described as "not the reassuring point you think it is".

**5. Any other business.**

None. The meeting closed at 10:41. The Chairman remained for eleven minutes
discussing the sandwiches with the catering staff, who have asked not to be
minuted by name.

---

*Minutes approved by the Chair. Not approved by the Chairman, who has not read
them and considers the meeting a success.*
