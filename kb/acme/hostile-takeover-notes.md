# ACME — Project BEETLE

*Filed by the office. Opening state when filed: unread. Opening state now:
unread.*

## Letter one — 6 June

> Dear Mr Beeblebrox,
>
> We are pleased to confirm ACME Corporation's non-binding interest in acquiring
> the Beeblebrox Group, in a transaction we are calling **Project BEETLE**.
>
> For clarity: an acquisition means ACME would own the Group, rather than the
> Group owning ACME. We recognise this requires a period of adjustment.
>
> Our advisors will contact your advisors. If you have no advisors, ACME is
> willing, as a courtesy, to supply you with three.

## Letter two — 20 June

> Dear Mr Beeblebrox,
>
> Thank you for your letter of 12 June, which consisted of a photograph of a
> caravan and the word "no".
>
> We have logged your response as **interest with conditions**. Our offer remains
> open. For reference, an offer is a sum of money proposed in exchange for
> ownership; ownership is the thing that is being discussed.

## Letter three — 2 July

> Dear Mr Beeblebrox,
>
> Following the announcement in the local paper that you have "sold the company
> to a car park", we must clarify that no sale has occurred. The car park is
> owned by a subsidiary of ACME, which is a coincidence we can explain at the
> meeting.
>
> The meeting is at 10:00 on Tuesday. The agenda is: (1) the acquisition;
> (2) the car park; (3) your caravan.
>
> Yours in anticipation,
> **ACME Corporation, Mergers and Very Acquisitions**

## Office note

The recipient believes these are invoices. The caravan is fine.
